typdedef uct_sci_structname_t

variable of that type is then typically called structname

The rest of the codebase has no special naming conventionn for static functions.
They are also named WITH the prefix just like externally linked functions.
Makes sense to follow the same scheme. Having a special naming convention for a function is nice, but it's not a big deal.


The struct uct_sci_iface_t is therefore just referred to as iface.
When the top most "class" uct_iface_h needs to be referred to tl_iface will be used as the variable name

If we ever need to refer to uct_base_iface_t, we'll just call it base_iface. That happens rarely though.
Other transports sometimes call it just "iface" but that is a mess!
